How it's made

No factory. Each batch goes from fruit to bottle in four steps.

  1. Choose the fruit

    Ripe, local avocados, picked by hand.

  2. Extract the oil

    The oil is taken from the fruit and left to settle until it is clear.

  3. Infuse with herbs

    Some oils rest with herbs for weeks to add scent and care.

  4. Blend and bottle

    The oil becomes butters, serums, balms and soaps, labelled by hand.
